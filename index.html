HOW TO PLAY

  • STEER WITH ARROWS / WASD (OR SWIPE / D-PAD ON TOUCH).
  • EAT DOTS FOR POINTS. THEY DON'T BITE BACK.
  • POWER PELLETS (THE BIG BLINKING ONES) MAKE YOU THE HUNTER FOR 9 SECONDS. YOUR RIVAL TURNS BLUE AND SCARED.
  • TOUCH A BLUE RIVAL TO CHOMP THEM. FIRST TO 3 CATCHES WINS.
  • STEAL THE POWER: A SCARED PAC-MAN CAN GRAB ANOTHER PELLET AND FLIP THE HUNT. IF BOTH ARE POWERED, YOU SLIP PAST EACH OTHER.
  • PELLETS GROW BACK AFTER ~11 SECONDS. THE TUNNELS WRAP AROUND.
  • ROOMS: ONE PLAYER CREATES A ROOM AND SHARES THE 4 RUNES (OR THE LINK). THE HOST'S BROWSER RUNS THE GAME - KEEP THAT TAB VISIBLE.
  • M MUTES SOUND.
